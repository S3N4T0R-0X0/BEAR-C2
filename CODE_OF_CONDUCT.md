# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, respectful, and responsible community around BEAR-C2, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, or religion.

We also pledge to use this project **only for legitimate educational, research, and authorized security testing purposes**.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its educational goals
- Respecting the legal and ethical boundaries of the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without explicit permission
- Using or encouraging the use of BEAR-C2 for unauthorized, illegal, or malicious purposes
- Discussing or sharing techniques intended for real-world unauthorized attacks
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or educational setting

---

## Project-Specific Rules

Because BEAR-C2 is an adversary simulation framework:

1. **Authorized Use Only**  
   All discussions, contributions, and testing must remain within legal and authorized boundaries (labs, educational environments, and explicitly authorized engagements).

2. **No Malicious Intent**  
   Any contribution or discussion that promotes or enables unauthorized or illegal activity will not be tolerated.

3. **Responsible Disclosure**  
   Security issues must be reported privately to the project owner (see [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/S3N4T0R-0X0/BEAR-C2/blob/main/SECURITY.md)).

4. **Attribution**  
   Proper credit must be given to the original author (ABDULREHMAN ALI / S3N4T0R-0X0) as required by the project license.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The project owner is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.  
They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub repository, issues, discussions) and also applies when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement Guidelines

The project owner will follow these guidelines when determining consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Correction**  
   Private warning and clarification of the nature of the violation.

2. **Warning**  
   A warning with consequences for continued behavior.

3. **Temporary Ban**  
   Temporary ban from interaction within the project spaces.

4. **Permanent Ban**  
   Permanent ban from any form of interaction within the project community.
